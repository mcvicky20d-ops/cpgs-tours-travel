FAQ

Common questions, answered

How do I book a tour package with CPGS?

The fastest way is WhatsApp: message +91 94874 11599 with your destination, dates and traveller count — or use the booking form above. We reply with the full itinerary and final per-person price.

How is the package price calculated?

All packages are per person on twin sharing — Goa from ₹5,999 (3N/4D), Manali from ₹17,500 (4N/5D), Chardham Yatra from ₹11,399. Hotel, breakfast and sightseeing transfers are included; flights/trains to the start point are extra. Full quote before you confirm.

Do you arrange group tours and pilgrimages?

Yes — group departures for Chardham Yatra plus group pricing for families, office outings, weddings and college tours, with tempo travellers and buses for larger groups.

Can a package be customized?

Every package is a starting point. Hotels, days, sightseeing and dates are planned around your group — send your plan on WhatsApp and we'll re-quote it.

Do you handle international trips like Malaysia and Bali?

Yes. Malaysia from ₹17,999 (3N/4D) and Bali from ₹27,500 (4N/5D) include hotels, transfers and city tours with visa guidance. Flights quoted per your departure city.
