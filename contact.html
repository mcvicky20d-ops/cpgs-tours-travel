FAQ

Common questions, answered

How do I book a trip with CPGS?

The fastest way is WhatsApp: message +91 94874 11599 with your route, dates, pickup point and number of travellers — or use the booking form above, which sends everything to WhatsApp for you. You can also call us directly.

How is the trip price calculated?

Local trips are priced by package (for example 8 hours / 80 km) and outstation trips per kilometre with a daily minimum, plus driver bata. Tolls, parking and permits are at actuals. You always get the full quote before confirming.

Do you handle group pilgrimages and office trips?

Yes — 12–17 seater tempo travellers for pilgrimages, weddings, college tours and corporate outings, plus sedans and SUVs for smaller groups.

Can packages be customized?

Every package is a starting point. Routes, stops, hotels, darshan timings and duration are planned around your group — just tell us what you have in mind.

Do you do late-night airport pickups?

Yes, we operate 24/7 including late-night and early-morning transfers. A night driving allowance may apply between 10 PM and 6 AM.
