Best Time to Visit Manali (2026 Month-by-Month Guide)

By CPGS Tours & Travels · Updated 17 July 2026

The best time to visit Manali is October to June. Go between December and February for snowfall, or between March and June for pleasant 10–25°C days when all adventure activities are open. Avoid July to September, when monsoon landslides can block the hill roads.

Month-by-month quick guide

December–January is peak snow season — book hotels early and pack serious winter layers. April–June is the classic family window: Rohtang and the Atal Tunnel are accessible, Solang Valley runs paragliding and zorbing, and the weather is perfect for sightseeing. October–November is the insider's pick — autumn colours, empty viewpoints and off-season hotel rates.

How many days do you need?

Five days (4 nights) covers Manali comfortably: Solang Valley, Rohtang/Atal Tunnel, Hadimba Temple, Old Manali and a Kullu valley excursion. That is exactly what our package covers at ₹17,500 per person including hotel, breakfast and sightseeing cab.
