Passport, Visa & FAQs

Everything you need to know — passport & visa help, group tours and honeymoon packages. Still have a question? We're one WhatsApp message away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CPGS help with passport application and renewal?

Yes. We guide you through fresh passport applications and renewals — the exact document checklist, filling the online Passport Seva form correctly, photo requirements and booking your appointment slot.

Which countries need a visa for Indian travellers, and do you assist?

For our international packages, Malaysia requires a tourist e-visa / eNTRI (applied online) and Indonesia (Bali) offers visa-on-arrival or e-VOA. We help you apply online, arrange the documents and photos, and pay the correct fee.

What documents are needed for a tourist visa?

Generally: a passport valid for at least 6 months, recent passport-size photos, confirmed return flight and hotel booking, and a recent bank statement. CPGS gives you the exact list for your destination before you apply.

How early should I apply for passport or visa?

Apply for a fresh passport at least 4–6 weeks before travel, and a tourist visa 2–3 weeks before departure. During peak season we recommend starting earlier — we help you plan the timeline.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

We provide complete documentation and application assistance, but the final approval and passport/visa issuance is at the sole discretion of the passport office or embassy. Government fees are paid directly to the authority.

What is the minimum group size for a group tour?

We arrange group tours from around 8 travellers upward. A 12–17 seater tempo traveller suits mid-size groups, and we provide mini-buses or full coaches for larger pilgrimage, corporate or wedding groups.

What kinds of groups do you handle?

Family reunions, corporate and office outings, college and school tours, wedding groups and temple pilgrimage batches — including Chardham Yatra group departures.

Do you offer special rates for groups?

Yes. Group bookings get discounted per-person rates and a dedicated coordinator. Send your group size and destination on WhatsApp for a custom group quote.

Can the group itinerary be customised?

Absolutely. Route, hotel category, meal plan, sightseeing and pace are all planned around your group's budget and interests, with GST-friendly billing for companies.

What is included in a group package?

Group transport, accommodation, daily breakfast (and more meals on request), planned sightseeing and an on-trip coordinator. We tailor inclusions to your budget.

Which are your most popular honeymoon destinations?

Bali, Jammu & Kashmir, Munnar, Kerala backwaters and the Andaman Islands are our most-loved honeymoon choices — from snowy valleys to private beaches and rice-terrace villas.

Do honeymoon packages include special arrangements?

On request we arrange romantic touches such as a candlelight dinner, flower/room decoration, a private cab and couple-friendly stays — just tell us what you'd like.

What does a honeymoon package cost?

Per-person prices start around ₹14,400 for Kashmir (4N/5D) and ₹17,500 for Munnar, up to ₹27,500 for Bali (4N/5D). Final price depends on hotel category, season and dates.

Can you customise a honeymoon itinerary?

Yes — we plan the number of days, hotels, sightseeing and private transfers entirely around the two of you. Share your dates and dream destination on WhatsApp.

How do we book a honeymoon package?

Message us on WhatsApp with your destination and travel dates. We reply with a romantic itinerary and a clear final quote before you confirm.
