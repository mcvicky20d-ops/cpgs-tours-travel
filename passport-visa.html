FAQ

Passport & Visa — Common Questions

Does CPGS Tours & Travels help with passport and visa?

Yes. CPGS assists travellers with fresh passport applications, passport renewal guidance and tourist visa applications for our international destinations such as Malaysia and Bali (Indonesia). We help you prepare the document checklist, fill the forms correctly and book the appointment.

What documents are needed for a passport application in India?

For a fresh passport you generally need proof of identity and address (Aadhaar, voter ID or driving licence), date-of-birth proof, recent passport-size photographs and the online application (ARN) receipt. CPGS shares an exact checklist for your case before your Passport Seva appointment.

Do I need a visa for Malaysia and Bali?

Indian passport holders need a tourist visa for Malaysia (e-visa / eNTRI available online) and Indonesia offers visa-on-arrival or e-VOA for Bali. CPGS guides you through the online application, fees and photo/document requirements for both.

How long does a visa take to process?

Malaysia e-visa is typically processed in 2–5 working days and Bali visa-on-arrival is issued at the airport (e-VOA can be done online before travel). We recommend applying at least 2 weeks before departure. Timelines vary by season, so apply early.

Can you apply for the visa on my behalf?

We provide end-to-end assistance: document check, form filling and appointment/e-visa submission guidance. Government fees are paid directly to the authority. Message us on WhatsApp with your travel dates and we will tell you exactly what is required.
