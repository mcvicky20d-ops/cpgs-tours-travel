Goa 3-Day Itinerary Under ₹6,000 (2026 Budget Plan)

By CPGS Tours & Travels · Updated 17 July 2026

Three nights is enough for a first Goa trip. This 3N/4D itinerary covers North Goa's beaches, Old Goa's churches, a sunset river cruise and South Goa's quiet sands — and with the CPGS package it costs just ₹5,999 per person with hotel, breakfast and transfers included.

Day 1 — Arrive + North Goa beaches

Land, drop bags, head straight to Baga and Calangute. Water sports till sunset, then the Tito's Lane strip or a quiet shack dinner. If it's a Saturday, the Arpora night market is unmissable.

Day 2 — Old Goa + Panjim + river cruise

Morning at the Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral (UNESCO sites), afternoon walking Fontainhas' colourful Latin Quarter, evening on a Mandovi river sunset cruise with live music.

Day 3 — South Goa day

Slow morning at Palolem or Colva — calmer water, whiter sand — with a stop at Fort Aguada on the way back for the lighthouse view.

Day 4 — Departure

Breakfast, last beach walk, souvenir stop for cashews and feni, airport drop.
