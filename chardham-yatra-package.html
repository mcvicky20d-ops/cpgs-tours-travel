FAQ

Chardham Yatra — Common Questions

How much does the Chardham Yatra package cost in 2026?

CPGS Chardham Yatra packages in 2026 start at Rs 11,399 per person for Ek Dham (3N/4D), Rs 13,800 for Do Dham (4N/5D) and Rs 24,000 for the full Char Dham (8N/9D). Extended-duration options are also available. Prices include stay, travel and darshan planning.

How many days are needed for the Char Dham Yatra?

The full Char Dham Yatra (Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath) needs 9 to 11 days from Haridwar/Rishikesh. CPGS offers an 8N/9D package at Rs 24,000 and a relaxed 10N/11D package at Rs 27,600 per person.

What is the difference between Ek Dham, Do Dham and Char Dham?

Ek Dham covers one shrine of your choice (usually Kedarnath or Badrinath), Do Dham covers two (typically Kedarnath + Badrinath), and Char Dham covers all four shrines — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath — in the traditional west-to-east order.

When is the Chardham Yatra open in 2026?

The Chardham shrines open around late April/May 2026 (Akshaya Tritiya) and close around Diwali/Bhai Dooj (October–November). May–June and September are the best months; avoid peak monsoon in July–August.

Is the Chardham Yatra package suitable for senior citizens?

Yes. CPGS plans senior-friendly pacing with acclimatisation halts, and can arrange pony/palki or helicopter options for the Kedarnath and Yamunotri treks at extra cost. Carry a basic health certificate as required by Uttarakhand authorities.

What is included in the Chardham package?

Accommodation at each halt, travel in a comfortable vehicle from Haridwar/Rishikesh, driver who knows the yatra route, darshan planning and CPGS 24x7 trip support. Meals beyond breakfast, helicopter/pony charges and personal expenses are extra.
