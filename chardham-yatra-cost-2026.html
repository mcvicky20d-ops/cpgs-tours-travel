Chardham Yatra Cost 2026: Ek Dham vs Do Dham vs Char Dham

By CPGS Tours & Travels · Updated 17 July 2026

Chardham Yatra packages cost between ₹11,399 and ₹27,600 per person in 2026, depending on how many shrines you visit and how many days you take. Ek Dham starts at ₹11,399 (3N/4D), Do Dham at ₹13,800 (4N/5D) and the complete Char Dham at ₹24,000 (8N/9D).

Which one should you choose?

Choose Ek Dham if you have a long weekend and one shrine calling you — most travellers pick Kedarnath or Badrinath. Choose Do Dham if you want the two most powerful darshans in one trip. Choose the full Char Dham if this is your once-in-a-lifetime yatra — and take the 10N/11D version if anyone in your group is above 55, because the extra days make the Kedarnath trek far easier.

What the price includes

Every CPGS Chardham package includes night-halt accommodation, travel from Haridwar/Rishikesh in a comfortable vehicle, a yatra-experienced driver and darshan planning. Helicopter or pony for the Kedarnath climb, meals beyond breakfast and your train/flight to Haridwar are extra.

When to go in 2026

The shrines open around late April/May 2026 and close after Diwali. May–June and September are the best windows — clear roads and manageable crowds. Avoid the July–August monsoon when landslides can close the route.
